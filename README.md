# கோலம் போடு — Kolam Podu

*"To draw a kolam"*

A mobile puzzle game rooted in the Tamil tradition of kolam — the intricate geometric floor patterns drawn each morning at the thresholds of homes across Tamil Nadu and the Tamil diaspora.

---

## What is a Kolam?

**Kolams (கோலம்)** are geometric patterns drawn with rice flour at the entrance of homes as a daily sunrise ritual. They are:

- Drawn freehand, without lifting the finger from the surface
- Structured around a grid of dots — **புள்ளி கோலம் (pulli kolam)**
- Inherently mathematical: built on symmetry, loops, and continuous paths
- Ephemeral by design — swept away and redrawn each day

The game takes this living tradition and builds an accessible, meditative puzzle experience around it.

---

## The Game

**Kolam Podu** gives players a dot grid and a single task: trace a continuous path that orbits the dots and closes into a complete pattern — the way a kolam is actually drawn.

### Core Loop

1. A dot grid appears
2. You draw a continuous line orbiting the dots — the line cannot be lifted mid-stroke
3. As you draw, the pattern blooms through live symmetry mirroring
4. The loop closes — the kolam settles, glowing like powder on stone

### What Makes It Special

| Feature | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Live symmetry** | Your stroke mirrors across axes in real time — one small movement becomes a full pattern |
| **Stroke economy** | Mastery means drawing more with fewer strokes — the true skill ceiling |
| **Daily kolam** | One new pattern every morning, shared across all players — a digital version of the real ritual |
| **Festival calendar** | Special patterns tied to Tamil festivals: Pongal, Karthigai Deepam, Thai Pongal, Navaratri |
| **Living gallery** | Every completed kolam is preserved — your personal collection of floor art |
| **Dot weaving** | Advanced mechanic where lines pass over and under specific dots, layering the pattern |

## Design Philosophy

### Culturally Grounded, Not Decorative
Colors, textures, patterns, and sound are drawn from real Tamil kolam tradition — not approximations. Each pattern in the game is rooted in actual kolam practice, with context about its meaning and use.

### Non-Predatory by Design
No energy systems. No paywalled levels. No manipulative push notifications. The daily kolam creates a natural return rhythm without coercion — the same way the real ritual does. Progression is earned through understanding, not time gates.

### Easy to Play, Hard to Master
The first pattern takes seconds to understand. Drawing a perfect stroke-economy score on a 7×7 grid with 8-axis symmetry is a genuine challenge requiring spatial reasoning and planning.

### Meditative UX
The experience should feel like the real thing: quiet, tactile, and satisfying. Completion animations settle like powder on stone. Sound design draws from traditional instruments. The UI gets out of the way.

## Progression

| Phase | Grid Size | Mechanics |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations (1–20) | 3×3 | Core loop, full guide, 2-axis symmetry |
| Practice (21–60) | 5×5 | Fading guide, stroke economy scoring |
| Craft (61–120) | 5×5 – 7×7 | 4-axis symmetry, dot weaving |
| Mastery (121+) | Free grid | 8-axis symmetry, no guide, full stroke economy |

## Cultural Note

Kolam is a living tradition practiced daily across Tamil Nadu and the Tamil diaspora. Drawing a kolam at the threshold of your home each morning is an act of welcome — to guests, to the goddess Lakshmi, to the day itself. This game is made with respect for that tradition.
